# Offboard

Offboard is a free chess club for people working in tech in and around Vilnius. Chess is the recurring reason to show up; meeting people is the point.

## Language

**Club**:
The Offboard community as a whole: its members, its gatherings and its shared history.
_Avoid_: League, platform, Vilnius Tech Chess

**Member**:
A person who works in tech in or around Vilnius and has joined the Club under their real name and photo and connected the chess account they play Matchdays with. Working in tech is self-declared, not verified.
_Avoid_: User, player, account

**Profile**:
What a Member shows other Members about themselves: their real name and photo, and optionally their company, position and what they are open to.
_Avoid_: Account, bio, card

**Company**:
The employer a Member states on their Profile, identified by its website: Members who give the same website belong to the same Company. Stating one is optional and is not verified.
_Avoid_: Team, employer, organisation

**Matchday**:
The Club's weekly online tournament, held every Sunday evening, in which every participating Member plays five games.
_Avoid_: Tournament, round, event, game night

**Season**:
Six consecutive Matchdays. A Member's Season score is the sum of their best four Matchday scores, and a Member may join at any point.
_Avoid_: League, series, cycle

**Season champion**:
The Member with the highest Season score when a Season ends. The title carries no prize.
_Avoid_: Winner, title holder

**Game**:
One chess game between two Members played as part of a Matchday. Games played elsewhere are not part of the Club's record.
_Avoid_: Match, pairing

**Leaderboard**:
A ranking of Members. There are three: per Matchday (by score that night), per Season (by Season score) and all-time (by Matchdays played).
_Avoid_: Standings, table, ranking

**Streak**:
The number of consecutive Matchdays a Member has played up to now.
_Avoid_: Run, attendance record

**Recap**:
The summary published after each Matchday: its Leaderboard and its Awards.
_Avoid_: Ledger, digest, newsletter

**Award**:
A recognition given in a Recap for something any Member could achieve regardless of rating, such as the biggest upset.
_Avoid_: Prize, trophy, badge

**Meetup**:
An in-person gathering of Members to play over the board. Not part of the first Seasons.
_Avoid_: Battle, event, IRL session

**Organiser**:
The single person who runs the Club and can remove a Member who does not belong.
_Avoid_: Admin, moderator, host
