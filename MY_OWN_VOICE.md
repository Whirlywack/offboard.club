# MY_OWN_VOICE for Offboard

## Voice samples
> Working in tech keeps you busy, and busy gets lonely. We think that's worth fixing. So every Sunday evening we play chess, five Games against other people in tech. With luck some of us become friends, and end up across a real board.

## Reader
Someone who works in tech in or around Vilnius, busier every year and short of time to make new friends. Some play chess often, some hardly at all.

## Product truth
Offboard is a free chess club for people who work in tech, and it exists so that they make new friends. Chess is how they meet.
Mechanism: five Games against other Members every Sunday at 20:00 on Lichess, a Profile that says where each Member works and what they're open to, and a Telegram group to talk in. Meeting and playing in person is the hope. Offboard does not organise that yet.

## Verified proof
- It is free. (source: the site takes no payment anywhere)
- It is run by one volunteer, the Organiser. (source: Privacy and terms)
- Nothing may be claimed yet about how many Members there are, friendships made or evenings held in person: no Matchday had been played on 26 November 2026.

## Posture and register
Friendly and human. The Club speaks as "we" and counts itself among the people playing ("some of us"); it never says "I". Two registers:
- Where the Club says why it exists (the home page, link previews, invitations): a little like a manifesto for making people in tech less lonely. Short. Never like a tech startup.
- Everywhere else (the sign-up steps, forms, errors, Privacy and terms, the Organiser's pages): plain, correct English, as short as it can be and still tell the reader what to do or what they agree to.

British spelling. The Club's own words are in `GLOSSARY.md` and keep their capitals (Member, Matchday, Season, Recap, Award, Profile, Organiser, Company, Game).

## Never say
- Startup or growth talk.
- An explanation of something the reader can already see, or the same thing twice.

## Always keep
- The words of the consent box at sign-up change only together with "Public listing" in Privacy and terms, and with a new "Last changed" date there.
- Every fact Privacy and terms states. Reword one; do not drop one.
- "Offboard isn't affiliated with Lichess or LinkedIn."
- Meeting in person stays a hope until Offboard runs such an evening.
