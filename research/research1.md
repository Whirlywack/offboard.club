Vilnius Tech Chess — Season 1 Rulebook

A low-ego, online-first chess league for engineers, designers, founders, and operators in Vilnius. Built to reward showing up, spark inter-company rivalries, and bridge winter online games into summer park boards.

1. Eligibility & Onboarding

⚬ Who can play: Anyone working in tech, startups, design, product, data, or indie building in or around Vilnius. All ratings welcome—roughly half the field sits between 800 and 1300 Elo.
⚬ Real identities only: Players register via LinkedIn (real name and photo) and link a verified Lichess account via OAuth. Anonymous accounts and alt handles are not eligible for standings.
⚬ Mid-season entry: New players can join at any point during a 6-week season without penalty.

2. Season Cadence & Weekly Swiss Format

Seasons run for 6 consecutive weeks to keep standings tight and give newcomers frequent fresh starts.

Parameter	Rule
Schedule	Every Sunday at 20:00 EET (~75 minutes total)
Platform	Private Vilnius Tech Chess team on Lichess
Format	5-Round Swiss (Dutch pairing system—no eliminations, never play the same opponent twice)
Time Control	5+3 Blitz (5 minutes + 3-second increment per move)
Match Scoring	Win = 1 pt, Draw = 0.5 pt, Loss = 0 pt, Unpaired Bye = 1 pt
Tiebreaks	Sonneborn-Berger (sum of defeated opponents' scores + half of drawn opponents' scores)
Early Draws	Agreed draws are disabled before Move 30 (Lichess Swiss default)

Practical Attendance Rules

⚬ Best 4 of 6 Scoring: Your individual seasonal score is the sum of your 4 highest weekly finishes. You can miss up to two Sundays for travel, family, or burnout without hurting your season.
⚬ Late Joins: Running 10 minutes late? Lichess allows late entry until more than half the rounds have started (meaning before Round 3 begins in a 5-round Swiss) and automatically awards a single 0.5-point bye for missed rounds.
⚬ Pausing & No-Shows: If something comes up mid-tournament, click Pause/Withdraw before the next round pairs. Signing up and letting your clock run out forfeits the game, automatically withdraws you from the event, and triggers Lichess's temporary Swiss restriction for repeat no-shows.
⚬ Teammate Avoidance: Before Round 1 locks, the tournament sync automatically passes same-company pairs into Lichess's native Forbidden Pairings list (where roster sizes allow) so you meet players from other Vilnius companies instead of your desk neighbor.

3. Individual Standings & Rating Brackets

Everyone plays together in one unified weekly Swiss, but seasonal prizes are split into four rating brackets so every skill tier has a genuine title race:

⚬ Open Division (All ratings)
⚬ Under 1500
⚬ Under 1200
⚬ Under 1000

Bracket Lock Rule: Your division is locked based on your Lichess Blitz rating at the start of your first tournament of the season (unrated/provisional accounts are placed after Week 1). If you start at 1140 and climb to 1260 by Week 5, you stay eligible to win Under 1200 for the rest of the season.

4. Company & Startup Cup ("You Can't Hurt the Team")

Company pride drives the league, without punishing teams for recruiting casual beginners.

⚬ Weekly Team Score: Each week, a company's score is the sum of its Top 3 individual scorers (Players #4 and #5 act as tiebreakers). A 900-Elo teammate who scores 1/5 can never drag down the company average—they can only add depth.
⚬ No Minimum Roster to Play: Even a solo employee scores points for their company; the leaderboard highlights squads that are "1 player short of a full 3-person roster" each week.
⚬ Indie / Founders Collective: Freelancers, solo builders, and between-roles players compete together under the Vilnius Indies banner.
⚬ Joint Startup Teams: Borrowing from Vilnius's corporate league model, two small companies (under 20 employees each) may register as an allied squad for the season.
⚬ Small Company Trophy: A dedicated seasonal award recognizes the highest-finishing company with fewer than 50 Vilnius employees, preventing 500-person enterprises from monopolizing the podium.

5. Weekly "Ledger" Digest & Non-Elo Awards

Within 15 minutes of Round 5 ending, the site and Telegram/Discord group publish the automated Matchday Ledger featuring weekly standings, two tactical puzzles pulled from actual Vilnius blunders that night, and awards anyone can win:

⚬ Biggest Upset: Largest Elo gap conquered in a single game.
⚬ Heart Attack Win: Decisive victory with the least time remaining on the clock (parsed from Lichess move timestamps).
⚬ Fastest Checkmate: Shortest checkmate of the night.
⚬ Game of the Month (Community Vote): At the end of Weeks 3 and 6, players vote in chat on 3 nominated games—chaotic 1000-Elo sacrifices regularly beat clean 2000-Elo endgames.
⚬ Longevity Milestones: Profiles track permanent lifetime Games Played and Attendance Streaks (badges unlocked at 25, 50, and 100 league games).

6. Season Finale & Summer IRL "Battles"

End-of-Season Meetup (Week 7)

Every 6-week online season wraps up with a casual offline evening at a Vilnius bar or park:

⚬ Division Awards & Streak Prizes: Small physical keepsakes (e.g., stamped coasters or engraved wooden pieces) handed out to bracket winners and anyone who played at least 4 of 6 weeks.
⚬ Top-4 Company Knockout: The top 4 companies from the online season play a fast 3v3 over-the-board semifinal and final (5+3). Tied matches go straight to a spectator-friendly Armageddon decider (White gets 5 minutes, Black gets 4 minutes and wins on a draw).

Summer Park & Bar "Battles" (May – September)

Outdoor meetups (e.g., Bernardine Garden or courtyard bars) ditch rigid Swiss rounds in favor of a drop-in Battle format:

⚬ 3-Hour Open Window: Arrive whenever you want, leave whenever you want. Challenge anyone available on a provided vinyl board using phone clocks (5+3 or 10+0).
⚬ Venue Password Check-In: A nightly 1-word password sits on the host table; entering it on the web app unlocks game logging for that evening.
⚬ The 2-Game Networking Cap: You can rematch a friend as many times as you like, but only your first 2 games against the same opponent count for points—nudging everyone to rotate seats and meet new faces.
⚬ Dual Points System:
  ⚬ Battle Points (Leaderboard): Win = 10 pts, Draw = 3 pts, Loss = 0 pts.
  ⚬ Club Points (Loyalty): Checking in = +50 pts, finishing any logged game = +10 pts. Club points accumulate across the summer regardless of win rate.
⚬ The "Herald" Rule: Every IRL session has one designated host whose sole job for the first 45 minutes is greeting solo arrivals and pairing them onto an open board immediately.

7. Fair Play & Etiquette

⚬ Zero external assistance: No engines, opening books, tablebases, or live coaching during games. Lichess's automated fair-play detection applies automatically; any flagged or closed Lichess account is removed from league standings and forfeits company points for that week.
⚬ No sandbagging: Intentionally tanking your Lichess rating before Season 1 to enter a lower bracket results in reassignment to the Open Division.
⚬ No recruiting pitches on the board: Work curiosity is encouraged; cold sales or aggressive recruiting pitches are not. Respect each player's "Open to..." profile tag.
